Ordering & quotes

How buying from a quote-based supplier works.

How does ordering work on DentalMart?

Browse the catalogue, add the items you need to your quote list, and send it in one request. We reply with pricing, bulk discounts, and availability for everything on your list. You can add both everyday consumables and large equipment to the same quote, then order once you're happy with the numbers.

Why don't I see prices on the products?

DentalMart is quote-based rather than fixed retail. Pricing on dental supplies and equipment varies with volume, account status, and current availability, so we quote each list directly to give clinics the best rate rather than a one-size-fits-all sticker price.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can request a quote for a single item or a full operatory build. Bulk and account pricing simply improve as your order grows.

Do I need an account to browse or request a quote?

No account is needed to browse the catalogue or send a quote request. We set up an account for you when you place your first order, so future ordering and pricing are faster.

Can I order supplies and equipment together?

Yes — this is one of the main reasons clinics use DentalMart. Put consumables, instruments, and operatory equipment on a single quote and a single invoice instead of managing several suppliers and shipments.

How long does a quote take?

We aim to reply to quote requests within one business day. Larger equipment or special-order items may take a little longer while we confirm manufacturer pricing and lead times.

How do I place an order once I have a quote?

Reply to your quote to confirm, and we'll arrange the invoice, payment, and shipping. If anything on the list changes, we can adjust the quote before you commit.

Licensing & safety

How we're licensed to sell medical devices in Canada.

Is DentalMart licensed to sell medical devices in Canada?

Yes. DentalMart operates under Wilk Solutions Corp, which holds a valid Health Canada Medical Device Establishment Licence (MDEL). Under Canada's Medical Devices Regulations, importers and distributors must hold an active MDEL to import and distribute medical devices of all classes in Canada — so you're buying from a properly licensed establishment.

What is an MDEL, and why does it matter?

A Medical Device Establishment Licence (MDEL) is issued by Health Canada to companies that import or distribute medical devices in Canada. It confirms the establishment maintains documented procedures for distribution records, complaint handling, recalls, and mandatory problem reporting — and it's renewed annually.

For a clinic, it means the supplier is accountable to Health Canada for how devices are handled, traced, and, if ever necessary, recalled.

Are the devices you sell Health Canada licensed?

As an MDEL holder we confirm that the medical devices we sell are licensed or authorized at the time of sale. Class II, III, and IV devices are covered by the manufacturer's Medical Device Licence (MDL), while the establishment distributing them holds the MDEL. The two work together.

How do you handle recalls or product complaints?

As part of MDEL compliance we maintain documented procedures for complaint handling, recalls, traceability of distributed products, and mandatory problem reporting to Health Canada. If you ever have an issue with a device, contact us and we'll act on it.
