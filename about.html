About DentalMart

DentalMart is the online supply store of Wilk Solutions — helping dental, medical, and veterinary clinics across Canada get the equipment and supplies they need, from one reliable source.

DentalMart is a Mississauga-based supplier serving clinics from coast to coast. From everyday consumables to handpieces, imaging, restorative materials, sterilization, and full operatory equipment, we bring it together under one roof so you can browse, build a quote list, and order from a single, dependable source — with bulk and account pricing and one invoice.

Part of the Wilk Solutions family

DentalMart is a Wilk Solutions company. Wilk Solutions is a Mississauga-based partner to dental, medical, and veterinary clinics across Canada, bringing clinic construction, equipment supply, and servicing together under one roof. With more than 35 years of experience, Wilk helps clinic owners open, equip, and run successful practices — from an empty unit to the first patient and well beyond.

Most clinic owners have to stitch together an architect, a contractor, and several equipment reps. Wilk Solutions exists to make that simpler: one experienced partner from the first floor plan onward, backed by 24/7 technical support and flexible financing through its partner Catalyst. That perspective — understanding clinics from the ground up, not just the products on a shelf — is what shapes what DentalMart stocks and how we support you after the boxes are unpacked.

What Wilk Solutions does

Through its family of companies, Wilk Solutions supports every stage of opening and running a clinic:

  • Clinic construction — custom clinic design, build-outs, and renovations, planned, permitted, and built to clinical standards for dental, veterinary, and medical spaces.
  • Equipment supply — operatory systems, sterilizers, handpieces, sensors, chairs, and imaging, sourced and installed. This is the catalogue you're browsing on DentalMart.
  • Servicing & support — preventive maintenance, repairs, and round-the-clock technical support to keep your clinic running.

The Wilk Solutions companies

Wilk Solutions brings together specialized companies that cover construction, supplies, and marketing, so clinic owners have one group to call:

  • DentalMart — the online store for dental supplies, instruments, and equipment (you're here).
  • BuiltByWilk — full-service clinic design and construction, built to Wilk Solutions standards.
  • MarketMyClinic — patient-growth marketing for dental, medical, and veterinary clinics: ads, websites, local SEO, and social.
  • ClinicLocator — real estate and procurement, helping owners find, evaluate, and secure the right clinic location.

Learn more about the group at wilksolutions.ca, or get an instant estimate for a clinic build or equipment project.
