About DentalMart

DentalMart is an online supply store for dental, medical, and veterinary clinics across Canada — everyday consumables through to full operatory equipment, in one catalogue with straightforward, quote-based pricing.

We bring the whole clinic supply list under one roof: consumables and infection control, handpieces, scalers, imaging, sterilization, restorative materials, and operatory equipment from names like Flight Dental Systems, DCI, Owandy, and BaseVac. Instead of juggling several suppliers and invoices, you browse the catalogue, add what you need to a quote list, and we come back with pricing, bulk discounts, and availability — one source, one invoice.

How ordering works

DentalMart runs on quotes rather than fixed retail prices, so clinics get pricing matched to their volume and account. Build a list across the catalogue, send it in a single request, and we handle the rest — including delivery across Canada and setup on larger equipment. No minimums, no account required to browse or request a quote.

Who we serve

We supply dental, veterinary, and medical clinics of every size — from single-chair practices restocking gloves to multi-operatory builds outfitting entire treatment rooms — coast to coast, shipped from Mississauga, Ontario.

Backed by Wilk Solutions

DentalMart is part of Wilk Solutions, a Mississauga-based group that has built, equipped, and serviced clinics across Canada for more than 35 years. That's the difference behind the catalogue: the team sourcing and supporting this equipment installs it in real operatories every week, so the guidance you get comes from people who know the gear in practice, not just on a shelf. If you're opening or expanding a clinic, Wilk's sister companies cover the whole project — construction, equipment, and marketing — so you have one group to call. You can get an instant clinic estimate or learn more at wilksolutions.ca.
